# PÁGINA 81 — “La invitación”

**Composición:** cinco paneles irregulares de tamaño medio y grande.  
**Ritmo:** reagrupación → revelación → resistencia → evaluación → decisión.  
**Función narrativa:** Ian revela la convocatoria y decide asistir solo. Nadie se retira todavía.

> **[ESTILO BASE] [IAN] [MATI] [JULIAN] [SEVERINE]**
>
> Full comic page layout inspired by layered psychological decision pages in modern American superhero comics. Use **five substantial irregular panels**, not an equal grid: one wide regrouping panel, one large private-message panel, two medium reaction panels, and one wide final decision panel. Thick black gutters, restrained red-black nightclub ambience, smoked glass, raw concrete, black steel, practical monitor light, expressive faces and body language. No dialogue, no captions, no speech bubbles, and no readable text.
>
> **Artistic execution:** unmistakably hand-drawn 2D American superhero-horror comic art. Strong organic black ink contours, visible variable line weight, deep brush-black shadows, selective cross-hatching on faces, hair, clothing, concrete, smoked glass, bandages, and monitor housings, flat restrained colors, hard-edged cel shading, subtle halftone print texture, matte paper finish. Never glossy 3D, cinematic CGI, plastic skin, game-engine lighting, or photorealistic rendering.
>
> **Visual direction:** modern restricted zone behind Severine’s enormous underground nightclub, connecting the private recovery room with the route-control area. Smoked-glass partitions, black steel framing, raw concrete, muted red spill from the club, practical surveillance screens, dark leather seating, and controlled industrial lighting. No Victorian décor, fantasy architecture, underground city, or cyberpunk holograms.
>
> **Character appearance lock:**
>
> - **Ian:** young human man with light hair, civilian glasses, dark practical jacket, shoulder bag, and green vector exo-gauntlets only. No helmet, no full exosuit, no military armor, no blue visor.
> - **Mati:** young human man with clearly human facial features, warm brown skin, handmade rustic purple proto-visor visibly cracked, dark civilian jacket with restrained purple accents. No helmet, no navy armor, no V.O.P.S. silhouette, no blue visor.
> - **Julián:** young human man with short curly fade, beard, dark navy civilian clothing, clean medical bandages, lightweight black wrist brace, faint bruising, no metal restraints. He has only just regained the ability to stand and remains weak.
> - **Severine:** young pale woman with straight black hair, elegant crimson dress, bare pale hands, controlled aristocratic posture. No white hair, no gloves, no wings, no bats.
>
> **Critical mixed-blood staff rule:** any background Nadir operators are nearly human-looking mixed-blood vampires with cool pale skin, subtle angularity, faint burgundy eye reflections, small discreet fangs only at certain angles, and unnaturally quiet posture. No monstrous anatomy, giant fangs, helmets, blue visors, or V.O.P.S.-style armor.
>
> **Panel 1 — regrouping:** just outside Julián’s recovery room, Severine steadies him after his first attempt to stand. One bare hand remains near his forearm or elbow. Julián is upright but visibly weak. Mati has just returned from the monitoring area and looks relieved. Ian returns from the darker corridor where he received the message, visibly more serious and withdrawn.
>
> **Panel 2 — Ian reveals the summons:** Ian shows only the minimum necessary on his phone. Cold corporate-blue light falls across his face, glasses, fingers, and the nearby red-black environment. The display contains only abstract secure-meeting shapes, a location marker, and a time-block structure. No readable name, logo, address, coordinates, or message.
>
> **Panel 3 — Mati objects:** Mati leans forward and gestures toward himself and Ian, making clear that Ian should not go alone. His cracked purple visor catches the cold blue reflection. Ian closes one hand around the phone and refuses through firm posture. No physical confrontation.
>
> **Panel 4 — Severine evaluates:** Severine studies Ian while exterior surveillance feeds glow behind her. Her expression suggests the invitation is dangerous and manipulative. She glances toward Julián and Mati, understanding that moving everyone together would expose them.
>
> **Panel 5 — final decision:** Ian stands slightly apart with the phone lowered and shoulder bag at his side. He has decided to attend alone. Mati remains opposed. Julián accepts the logic without enthusiasm. Severine shifts from debate toward planning. Two different route indications appear in the background, foreshadowing the next page. Nobody leaves yet.
>
> **Emotional goal:** Ian chooses to go because the sender already knows too much and the invitation is directed specifically at him. The decision is deliberate, resisted, and strategically understood.
>
> **Negative reinforcement:** Ian already leaving, all characters accepting instantly, service train already departing, Julián fully healthy, Julián with metal cuffs, Mati in V.O.P.S. armor, readable phone message, Norman physically present, equal five-panel grid, tiny reaction boxes, cyberpunk holograms, glossy 3D.
