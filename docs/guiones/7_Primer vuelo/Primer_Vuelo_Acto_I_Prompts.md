# PRIMER VUELO — ACTO I: EL INGENIERO
## Prompts de páginas completas para SeaArt

## ESTRUCTURA DEL ACTO

**Extensión propuesta:** 12 páginas.

**Ritmo visual:** llegada controlada → construcción de la identidad falsa → reconocimiento del circuito → descubrimiento de la tecnología de Ian → búsqueda de recursos → reclutamiento separado → primera alianza.

**Objetivo dramático del acto:** presentar a Ian como ingeniero e infiltrado, no como vigilante. El acto debe terminar con un equipo que acepta trabajar por dinero, mientras Ian oculta que su verdadero objetivo es acercarse al clan Kurogane.

## GIRO CEL-SHADING — PRIMER VUELO
### Pegar después de [ESTILO BASE] y [DISEÑO DE PÁGINA]

```text
PRIMER VUELO VISUAL IDENTITY — stronger graphic cel-shading direction. Push the artwork toward crisp premium digital comic coloring while preserving visible hand-inked contours. Use two or three clearly separated flat value shapes per surface, large angular shadow planes, sharp shadow borders, simplified controlled hatching, clean silhouette readability, and selective halftone only inside deep shadows or atmospheric backgrounds. Reduce scratchy rendering and excessive micro-texture on faces. Faces must remain expressive, youthful, clean, and recognizable through shape design rather than wrinkles or realistic pores.

Tokyo environments use clean graphic blocks of nocturnal cyan, violet, muted magenta, traffic red, sodium amber, concrete grey, and deep blue-black, always separated into distinct color areas rather than blended gradients. Neon may illuminate nearby objects through hard-edged colored shadow shapes, but never create a global neon wash. Vehicles use matte or satin painted surfaces with graphic reflections represented as simple flat shapes, never glossy 3D reflections. Technology uses precise planar highlights and restrained colored accents, not floating holographic clutter.

The visual contrast with Distrito Nulo must be noticeable: cleaner color separation, bolder shadow geometry, more elegant automotive shapes, more controlled inks, fewer dense cross-hatched surfaces, and a slightly sharper anime-influenced sense of speed and mechanical clarity without becoming manga or cel-animation. Still unmistakably a modern American superhero comic page. No photorealism, no painterly rendering, no plastic CGI, no smooth airbrush gradients.
```
