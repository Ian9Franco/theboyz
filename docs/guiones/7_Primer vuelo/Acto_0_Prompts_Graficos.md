# PRIMER VUELO — PRÓLOGO / ACTO 0
## Documento de Prompts Gráficos por Página

Este documento traduce el **Prólogo — La Torre** a prompts gráficos de página completa, listos para usar en SeaArt.

La meta de esta versión es mantener **continuidad narrativa real** entre páginas: mismos espacios, mismos objetos persistentes, mismas prendas, transiciones claras y un flujo visual donde cada página hereda algo de la anterior y entrega algo concreto a la siguiente.

---

## CÓMO USAR CADA PROMPT

Cada bloque se arma así:

**[ESTILO BASE] + [GIRO CEL-SHADING PRIMER VUELO] + [DISEÑO DE PÁGINA] + [LOCKS/FICHA DE LOS PERSONAJES DE ESA PÁGINA] + [PROMPT DE PÁGINA COMPLETA] + [NEGATIVO ESTÁNDAR]**

---

## ESTILO BASE (pegar siempre, sin cambios)

Official modern American comic book page, bold solid black panel borders, thick black gutters, dynamic full-page composition with one dominant cinematic panel plus smaller inset panels, zoom strips, overlapping frames, diagonal panel cuts, occasional figures breaking panel borders, strong perspective, expressive body language. Solid hand-inked line work, clean organic line art, expressive faces, clean skin with no wrinkles, flat cel-shading with defined shadow borders, halftone matte paper texture, matte finish. Accurate character skin tones, no global color wash. 2D vector-like illustration, sharp focus, traditional inked texture, flat colors, no glossy or metallic reflections, dry matte surfaces. No rain. Time continuity: this prologue takes place in Parker Tower during clean daytime interiors and late-afternoon corporate light. Bright glass architecture, controlled laboratory light, cool neutral interiors, no atmospheric grime. Only subtle city reflections on glass.

---

## GIRO CEL-SHADING PRIMER VUELO (pegar siempre, sin cambios)

Push this saga toward a cleaner and more graphic cel-shaded look than Distrito Nulo. Use harder and more geometric shadow shapes, clearer color separation, cleaner silhouettes, and more elegant modern comic rendering. Reduce excessive dirt, noise, and scratchiness. Keep the hand-inked identity, but make the finish more polished, more design-driven, and more readable. Parker Tower must feel premium, ordered, and technologically advanced: white architecture, smoked glass, graphite metals, cool cyan interface light, restrained magenta accents, subtle amber sunlight reflections. Characters should still feel hand-drawn, but with stronger color blocking and clearer visual hierarchy.

---

## DISEÑO DE PÁGINA (pegar siempre, sin cambios)

Page architecture inspired by modern superhero comic layouts: avoid simple equal-size grid pages. Use one clear dominant image per page, supported by smaller inset panels, stacked zoom strips, cinematic horizontal strips, and occasional borderless close-up inserts. Prefer bold black gutters and confident negative space. Characters may break out of panel borders when the action peaks. Use strong foreground silhouettes framing the scene, deep perspective corridors, low-angle and high-angle camera views, speed lines, impact bursts, and overlapping panels. For action pages, use sequential movement across panels like storyboard beats: wide establishing action, close-up mechanical detail, impact panel, reaction inset, final wide consequence. For emotional or noir pages, use a large atmospheric panel with small hand/eye/object insets. Allow stylized sound-effect lettering integrated into the background only when explicitly requested by the page prompt, as part of the art and not as dialogue. Use pure white or flat-color negative backgrounds for selected impact panels, with figures and energy breaking past the panel border.

## LIBRERÍA DE PERSONAJES / LOCKS

### [IAN-LAB]
Ian, early Vesper Wing before the mask era, in civilian engineering mode. Young slim man, light skin, light wavy hair, intelligent face, brown-framed glasses. No superhero suit, no mask, no cape, no exo-gauntlets. He wears practical modern civilian clothes suitable for a high-end lab: dark hoodie or dark overshirt, fitted neutral T-shirt, work pants or dark jeans, sneakers or practical boots. He carries a tablet, tools, cables, or prototype parts naturally. His body language is focused, analytical, understated. He should read as a brilliant engineer trusted inside Parker Tower, not as a vigilante.

### [JAZ-CIVIL]
Jaz / Jasmine in civilian mode. Young woman with sunset-gold hair, cat-eye glasses, sharp intelligent gaze, elegant modern styling. Dark fitted top or black turtleneck, sleek pants or skirt, minimalist accessories, refined urban look. She projects control, emotional intelligence, and strategic precision. She can appear in person inside Parker Tower or on a clean holographic call. No combat suit on this prologue.

### [MATI-CIVIL]
Mati in civilian/non-combat mode. Young man with warm brown skin, rounded expressive face, subtle dimples, short dark hair. He may wear a civilian jacket, tee, casual pants, practical sneakers. If the page needs his condition monitored, keep the unstable proto-visor available nearby or briefly worn during testing: rustic, handmade, purple-toned, slightly cracked. He is expressive, dryly funny, and human.

### [JULIAN-CIVIL]
Julián in civilian mode. Young man with olive skin, short curly dark hair in a fade, short beard, confident slightly arrogant expression. Casual stylish clothing: sweater, bomber, tee or overshirt, fitted pants, urban shoes. Restless hands, tactile curiosity, charismatic posture. No combat costume. No cosmic gear. If he interacts with tech, he does so like someone who touches things he was told not to touch.

### [UANDI-CIVIL]
Uandi in civilian mode. Very muscular man with medium skin tone, short dark hair, short beard, round glasses. He must keep his tattoo continuity: classic sleeve on left arm, clean right arm with two black bracelets/bands. Civilian clothing can include fitted black athletic T-shirt or sleeveless training top, cargo pants or practical joggers. He should look physically powerful but calm and patient. No transformed red thermal form in this prologue.

### [NORMAN]
Norman Parker. Distinguished middle-aged businessman, elegant, controlled, calm, authoritative. Refined face, carefully styled hair with subtle gray, premium tailored suit, tasteful tie, expensive shoes, immaculate grooming. He must feel like a polished mentor and visionary corporate leader, not openly sinister. His office should visually reinforce wealth, power, and trustworthiness: glass walls, skyline views, minimalist art, clean architecture, expensive furniture.

### [PARKER-TOWER]
Parker Tower is a premium corporate-tech environment. Bright glass facades, white or cool gray architecture, black structural accents, clean labs, prototype bays, premium conference rooms, panoramic city views, controlled cool lighting, subtle holographic interfaces, security glass, matte metals, and immaculate surfaces. It should feel aspirational and almost utopian from Ian’s point of view.

---

## NEGATIVO ESTÁNDAR (pegar siempre, sin cambios)

No speech bubbles, no captions, no readable text, no photorealism, no 3D render, no glossy concept-art sheen, no wet surfaces, no rain, no global orange or sepia filter, no random cyberpunk clutter, no dirty dystopian alley mood, no superhero suit for Ian, no cape, no mask, no exo-gauntlets, no combat poses unless explicitly requested, no duplicate characters, no extra teammates beyond those named, no de-aged cartoon babies, no old wrinkled faces, no metallic skin reflections, no AI interface text blocks large enough to become readable.

## PÁGINA 1 — DOS AÑOS DESPUÉS

**Personajes:** [IAN-LAB] [PARKER-TOWER]

**Prompt de página completa:**

Full comic page layout, opening prologue page establishing Parker Tower as Ian’s new normal. Use 5 panels with one dominant large establishing panel and four supporting insets. No dialogue, no captions, no speech bubbles, no readable text.

Exact story goal: nearly two years after Distrito Nulo, Ian now works as a trusted engineer inside Parker Tower. This page must immediately communicate stability, resources, advancement, and false safety. Ian is no longer improvising in a damaged hideout — he now has access to elite laboratories, advanced tools, and architectural scale.

Panel 1 — dominant establishing panel: huge cinematic interior of Parker Tower’s engineering lab. Bright daytime. Glass walls, skyline beyond, premium tech environment. Ian stands or walks at the center of a sleek workstation island surrounded by monitors, prototype modules, anomalous-energy test housings, adaptive armor samples, and defense-system mockups. He looks fully integrated into the space. Several technicians in the background treat him as someone important.

Panel 2 — close detail inset: Ian’s hand adjusting a prototype component, with clean geometric cel-shaded lighting and small hard shadow shapes. Show confidence and familiarity.

Panel 3 — small reaction panel: a technician or assistant consults Ian, and Ian answers with calm authority while still working. No readable screens.

Panel 4 — memory object inset: on a side shelf or near Ian’s station, show one or two personal remnants from the Dumbo era — a worn tool, a scorched component, maybe a small candid photo of the group. This is the emotional bridge to the past.

Panel 5 — final wide horizontal strip: Ian alone for a beat at the workstation after others move away, framed by the scale of the tower and the city outside. He looks quietly satisfied, but slightly distant.

Emotional goal: the page must feel like Ian has finally found infrastructure, legitimacy, and purpose.

Continuity exit: end with Ian still in the lab and the medical/monitoring area nearby, setting up the next page where the others arrive for testing.

## PÁGINA 2 — LOS PIBES YA NO SON UN EQUIPO

**Personajes:** [IAN-LAB] [JAZ-CIVIL] [MATI-CIVIL] [JULIAN-CIVIL] [UANDI-CIVIL] [PARKER-TOWER]

**Prompt de página completa:**

Full comic page layout, ensemble reunion page inside Parker Tower’s controlled testing area. Use 6 irregular panels with a large dominant central panel and several smaller reaction/detail insets. No dialogue, no captions, no speech bubbles, no readable text.

Exact continuity: this page happens immediately after page 1. Ian is overseeing routine monitoring and checkups related to the group’s transformations. This is not an action scene. It is a warm but slightly awkward reconnection showing chemistry without pretending they still function as a constant team.

Panel 1 — dominant central panel: the four friends gathered in a clinical-tech test zone inside Parker Tower. Ian is actively examining or scanning Mati while Jaz observes and Julián casually hovers near another station. Uandi waits nearby, large and patient. The space is clean, elegant, and well equipped.

Panel 2 — Mati check inset: close panel of Ian adjusting scanning gear or observing Mati’s eyes/visor-related condition. Mati looks annoyed or sarcastic.

Panel 3 — Julián trouble inset: Julián lightly touching or inspecting a prototype device he clearly should not be touching. Ian notices from the side.

Panel 4 — Uandi waiting inset: Uandi seated or standing with calm resignation, arms crossed or relaxed, clearly used to being tested. Keep his tattoos and round glasses visible.

Panel 5 — Jaz control inset: Jaz reviewing results on a tablet or holographic surface, composed and sharp, functioning as support and observer.

Panel 6 — group beat horizontal strip: all of them share the same frame for a brief human beat. Slight smiles, familiarity, and the sense that these people know each other deeply even if their routines are no longer aligned.

Emotional goal: affectionate distance. They still have chemistry, but the group is no longer living in constant sync.

Continuity exit: the tests are ending, which leads directly to the dispersal on the next page.

## PÁGINA 3 — CADA UNO SIGUE SU CAMINO

**Personajes:** [IAN-LAB] [JAZ-CIVIL] [MATI-CIVIL] [JULIAN-CIVIL] [UANDI-CIVIL] [PARKER-TOWER]

**Prompt de página completa:**

Full comic page layout, emotional transition page about separation after a shared routine. Use 5 panels: one large atmospheric panel, two medium reaction panels, and two small object/gesture insets. No dialogue, no captions, no speech bubbles, no readable text.

Exact continuity: immediately after the monitoring scene. The tests are over. The others prepare to leave Parker Tower and return to their own independent lives. The page must communicate that there is no dramatic breakup — only natural distance.

Panel 1 — dominant atmospheric panel: wide lobby or exit-adjacent area inside Parker Tower. The group is physically together but already separating. Mati is collecting his things, Julián is responding to a phone/message, Uandi is ready to go, Jaz is composed, and Ian is just slightly behind the rhythm, as if he wishes the moment would last a little longer.

Panel 2 — Mati departure inset: Mati slinging on a jacket or stepping away with a quick half-smile.

Panel 3 — Julián departure inset: Julián glancing at a phone or communicator, expression shifting toward elsewhere, then moving off.

Panel 4 — Uandi departure inset: Uandi giving a small quiet gesture before leaving, grounded and warm.

Panel 5 — final small silence panel: Ian remains in the foreground while the others recede into elevator or corridor depth. Jaz may remain behind or pause nearby. The perspective should emphasize the physical widening of space between them.

Emotional goal: no melodrama, only the quiet ache of changed routines.

Continuity exit: Ian and Jaz remain together after the others leave, directly setting up the next page.

## PÁGINA 5 — LA ANOMALÍA

**Personajes:** [IAN-LAB] [JAZ-CIVIL] [PARKER-TOWER]

**Prompt de página completa:**

Full comic page layout, inciting-incident page where Ian discovers his own tech in Tokyo race imagery. Use 6 panels with a strong sequential detective rhythm: quiet setup, image recognition, technical comparison, realization, Jaz reaction, final reveal. No dialogue, no captions, no speech bubbles, no readable text.

Exact continuity: immediately after page 4, still in Parker Tower. Ian is alone or almost alone at his station when a data review or anomaly scan flags material from Tokyo. The page must feel like the story truly begins here.

Panel 1 — setup panel: Ian seated or leaning at his workstation, reviewing system records or flagged external footage. Calm before the discovery.

Panel 2 — suspicious image inset: a vehicle from a Tokyo street-racing environment appears on one of the displays. Keep the screen content stylized and mostly image-based, not readable text.

Panel 3 — recognition detail panel: Ian zooms in on a very specific component — adaptive engine housing, smart armor lattice, or closed energy regulator. His body language tightens.

Panel 4 — technical comparison panel: split-style composition showing the Tokyo vehicle part on one side and Ian’s in-house prototype or design sample on the other. The match is unmistakable.

Panel 5 — Jaz reaction or re-entry panel: Jaz notices Ian’s sudden intensity and comes into the frame or leans toward the display.

Panel 6 — final reveal panel: the strongest reveal image of the page. The suspicious vehicle image dominates, paired with Ian’s shocked face in foreground or inset. This must communicate that his exact work is out there where it should not be.

Emotional goal: violated ownership and stunned recognition.

Continuity exit: Ian gathers the evidence and moves toward Norman’s office.

Panel 1 — dominant panel: Ian entering Norman’s office carrying a tablet, module case, or evidence pack. Norman stands or sits in a pristine high-level office with skyline behind him. He appears attentive and calm.

Panel 4 — Norman listens inset: close-up of Norman’s composed face. He should not look cartoonishly evil or overly alarmed.

Panel 5 — final wide strip: both men framed in the same office space, the city behind them, evidence between them. The power dynamic is subtle: Ian brings truth; Norman controls the room.

Panel 1 — dominant panel: Norman and Ian in a measured corporate conversation, seated or standing across from each other with the city beyond. Norman’s body language is open, paternal, and pragmatic.

Panel 2 — Ian proposes formal solutions inset: Ian gesturing toward internal security, official channels, or a larger investigation.

Panel 3 — Norman dismisses broader exposure inset: Norman calmly countering, emphasizing discretion through composed hand gestures and controlled posture.

## NOTA DE DIRECCIÓN VISUAL

Este prólogo debe sentirse visualmente distinto a Distrito Nulo:

- menos caos urbano;
- menos suciedad;
- menos desesperación física;
- más arquitectura limpia;
- más tecnología premium;
- más claridad compositiva;
- más cel-shading duro y elegante;
- emoción contenida, no melodrama.

La traición todavía no ocurrió a ojos de Ian. Por eso el mundo **debe parecer funcional y prometedor**.
